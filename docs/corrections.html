Corrections Policy

We aim to be accurate. If we get something wrong, we fix it and say so.

How we correct

  • Factual errors are corrected in the article as soon as they are verified, and the article's Last updated date changes.
  • Significant corrections are logged below with the date and what changed.
  • If a claim cannot be verified against a cited source, we soften it or remove it.
  • We will review and, where justified, remove or amend content about a named person or company on request, once the concern is identified and the sources checked.

How to report an error

Open an issue on our public tracker: https://github.com/joshuaofisrael/humanoid-news/issues
