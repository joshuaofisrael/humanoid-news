South Korea eases safety rules to put 20 humanoids on factory floors

By Humanoid News Staff · 14 October 2026

Short answer: South Korea has eased safety regulations originally written for fixed industrial robots, allowing mobile humanoid robots to work on factory floors. Twenty AI-powered bipedal humanoids from Korean developer Robros are set to be deployed, including at Nepes.

Existing rules were designed for stationary robot arms operating behind barriers, which did not fit robots that walk among workers. The change is one of the first regulatory moves anywhere aimed specifically at enabling humanoids in production environments.

The deployments span manufacturing settings across several industries, according to the Seoul Economic Daily.
