Brett Adcock posts Figure 03 campus walk video: "One day the entire world will look like this"

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 7 November 2026 · Last updated 7 November 2026

Short answer: On 6 November 2026, Figure founder and CEO Brett Adcock posted on X a video of humanoid robots walking through campus and office spaces, captioned: "One day the entire world will look like this." The post is a new viral clip and caption, separate from Figure's earlier 2–3 November BotQ campus-walk coverage.

In the 6 November post, Adcock wrote exactly: "One day the entire world will look like this." The attached video (about 86 seconds in the X media metadata) shows white-and-black bipedal humanoids moving through indoor corridors, lobbies and outdoor campus-style walkways. Adcock did not add production rates, delivery totals or customer names in that caption.

How this differs from the earlier BotQ walk clip

HumanoidBeat already covered a late-October/early-November Adcock video of completed Figure 03 units walking away from BotQ (Figure 03 robots leave BotQ on foot, 3 November 2026). That piece stressed the gap between a campus walk and disclosed factory output. This 6 November post is a different clip and a different framing: a short, forward-looking caption rather than a BotQ production claim.

Reach and reaction (as reported on X)

When this story was prepared, X public metrics on Adcock's post showed on the order of about 263,000 impressions, about 3,890 likes, about 624 replies and about 368 reposts. Those counters move quickly and are not independently audited. Public replies quoted in the wider X thread split between enthusiasm for a humanoid-filled workplace future and unease about the same scene; those are user reactions, not company statements.

What the post does not establish

Adcock's caption is a vision line, not a shipment tally. The video does not, by itself, state weekly BotQ output, how many of the robots shown are customer-bound, or whether every segment was fully autonomous. Readers should not treat the clip as proof of mass commercial deployment. For manufacturing context that remains separate from this caption, see our earlier BotQ walk report.

Caveats

Quote and link above are taken from Adcock's public X post. Impression and engagement figures are X platform metrics at scout time, not audited audience research. Scene descriptions summarise what appears in the posted video; they are not a factory tour or an independent product test.
