OpenAI folds its robotics team into core research but keeps a 2027 humanoid demo target

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 15 November 2026 · Last updated 15 November 2026

OpenAI no longer has a stand-alone robotics team. The company confirmed the reorganisation to Fortune, which reported it as an exclusive on 13 November, and the industry account The Humanoid Hub summarised the move on X early on Sunday, noting that OpenAI is still building its own humanoid with a first public demo targeted for 2027.

According to Fortune, the robotics research team now reports to chief research officer Mark Chen as part of the main research organisation, while the applied robotics and hardware teams have moved into the larger Applied organisation under president Greg Brockman. A company spokesperson told Fortune that robotics remains a core part of OpenAI's long-term vision and presented the change as a sign of commitment rather than a retreat.

Related reading

The stated reason is a bet that general-purpose models can control robots directly. Fortune reports that OpenAI researchers had expected at the start of the year to build on video models such as Sora, but that frontier models including GPT-6 Astra have since shown they can be applied to robotics. It cites an NVIDIA study in which an Astra-powered agent scored 74.8% on a simulated manipulation benchmark, against 51.0% for the best specialised robot models on that leaderboard. That result was in simulation, not on physical robots.

OpenAI wound down an earlier robotics team in 2020, Fortune notes. The timing was tight enough that OpenAI's registered booth at the CoRL robotics conference in Austin this week sat empty. The Decoder, which first described the move as a disbanding, updated its story after OpenAI clarified that robotics work continues.

Analysis

If general models really can drive robot control, the advantage shifts toward companies with robots already deployed and collecting site-specific data, a point MIT professor Russ Tedrake made to Fortune. OpenAI's 2027 humanoid demo is now the checkable milestone for its own version of that bet.
