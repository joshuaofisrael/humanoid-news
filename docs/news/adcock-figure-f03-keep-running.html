Adcock posts F.03 fleet video and says Figure plans to keep running the robots

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 8 November 2026 · Last updated 11 November 2026

Short answer: Figure chief executive Brett Adcock posted a 90-second video on 7 November 2026 captioned simply "F.03 humanoid robots". When a follower asked whether F.03 would eventually be decommissioned as the company works on its next robot, Adcock replied: "The plan is to keep running them, it's quite a good robot."

The question came from an X user who suggested Figure was close to finishing the design of a "Figure 4" and asked whether "Figure 3 eventually be decommissioned, or do you find it too valuable?" Adcock did not confirm anything about a successor's design or timing in his answer. He only addressed the fate of the current fleet.

Why the reply matters

Humanoid makers are iterating quickly, and early buyers and partners want to know whether hardware bought or deployed today will be supported once the next generation arrives. Adcock's answer suggests Figure intends to keep F.03 units in service rather than retire them on a fixed schedule.

What was not said

Adcock gave no numbers for the F.03 fleet, no deployment sites and no timeline for a next-generation robot in these posts. Any claim that a "Figure 4" is near completion comes from the follower's question, not from Figure. HumanoidBeat treats the video post and the reply as the public record.

Figure later posted a hiring notice for a night-shift coordinator to oversee humanoid robots.
