Hyundai Motor Group now owns all of Boston Dynamics after buying out SoftBank's last stake

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 18 November 2026 · Last updated 18 November 2026

Hyundai Motor Group has taken full ownership of Boston Dynamics, the maker of the Atlas humanoid, The Korea Herald reports. According to a third-quarter regulatory filing by Hyundai Glovis on Tuesday, all put and call options tied to Boston Dynamics' common shares were exercised and expired during the quarter.

The transaction completes the buyout of SoftBank's remaining 9.65% stake, after the Japanese group triggered its put option in July under an earlier shareholder agreement, the paper says. Existing shareholders, including Hyundai Motor Group executive chair Chung Euisun, HMG Global and Hyundai Glovis, bought the remaining shares, giving Chung and group affiliates 100% of the company.

Hyundai Glovis put in 46.5 billion won (about $34.8 million) as part of the deal, raising its stake by 1.08 percentage points to 12.47% at the end of the third quarter. That followed a 63.1 billion won investment in the first half of 2026.

The Korea Herald says Hyundai aims to build annual production capacity for 30,000 Atlas units by 2028 and to deploy them in its own plants, starting at Hyundai Motor Group Metaplant America in Georgia. The robots would first sort and move car parts before moving on to more complex assembly work once the technology is proven.

The paper also reports that Boston Dynamics' revenue for the first nine months of the year rose 29% to 136 billion won, while its net loss widened to 626.6 billion won because of heavy research and production spending. It cites sources suggesting a possible stock market listing around 2029 or 2030, but says no official timeline has been set.

Analysis

With no outside shareholders left, Hyundai can fund Atlas on its own schedule. The figures above show why that matters: Boston Dynamics is spending far more than it earns, and the 30,000-unit target depends on the parent continuing to pay for the ramp-up.

Note: Ownership and financial figures are as reported by The Korea Herald from a Hyundai Glovis filing; we have not seen a separate statement from Hyundai or Boston Dynamics.
