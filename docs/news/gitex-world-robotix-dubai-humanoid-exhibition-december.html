GITEX Global adds a dedicated robotics show in Dubai, with Unitree, UBTECH and Lumos humanoids on the list

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 16 November 2026 · Last updated 16 November 2026

GITEX Global will hold its first dedicated robotics exhibition, GITEX World RobotiX, at the Dubai Exhibition Centre in Expo City Dubai from 8 to 11 December, Robotics and Automation News reports. It will feature humanoid robots, autonomous mobile machines, quadrupeds, exoskeletons and robot development tools.

Named exhibitors include Unitree, UBTECH, AiMOGA, DEEP Robotics and UAE-based Micropolis Robotics. Chinese maker Lumos Robotics will give the Middle East debut of its MOS 2, a wheeled two-arm robot for industrial work that the company says can carry up to 50 kg and run for more than eight hours. Lumos will also run live demonstrations of its NIX S3 humanoid, which has 21 degrees of freedom.

Distributor Lootah Robotics plans to show machines from Unitree, AGIBOT, Pudu Robotics, UBTECH and EngineAI. On the computing side, JWIPC will introduce robot controllers based on NVIDIA's Thor module and Songying Technology will demonstrate its ORCA simulation and training software, according to the report.

The article cites Counterpoint Research figures putting global humanoid shipments above 22,000 units in the first half of 2026, up 300% year on year, with more than 50,000 expected by the end of the year. Those are the research firm's estimates.

GITEX World RobotiX is part of the 46th GITEX Global, which opens with a summit at Dubai World Trade Centre on 7 December. Organisers expect more than 6,000 exhibitors across the event and its associated shows.

Analysis

Trade shows mostly feature demonstrations rather than deployments. The Gulf is worth watching, though, as governments there fund pilots in hospitality and public services.
