Why Reward AI's OM-1 humanoid parcel-delivery and stairs demo is getting attention

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 28 October 2026 · Last updated 28 October 2026

Short answer: Robotics company Reward AI posted video on X on 27 October 2026 showing humanoid robots, controlled by its OM-1 model, taking parcels out of a vehicle for last-mile delivery and grasping objects while standing on stairs. The company says the clips are autonomous. By the morning of 28 October the main post had passed 320,000 views on X.

In follow-up posts, Reward AI said it had stress-tested the model on parcels placed in different positions and orientations, and that the robots adjust their approach, climb and descend stairs while holding the package. French site RoboActu, which covered the demo on 28 October, described how the robot adapts to a parcel's position before picking it up and moving off with the load.

OM-1 was introduced in September as the core of Reward AI's Omnibody system. According to the company, the model learns from people wearing a capture device, including a seven-degree-of-freedom glove called Omnibody Hand that records hand movement, contact and force, rather than from teleoperated robots, and the company says it does not use robot-collected data to train the general policy. Reward AI also says a new task can be learned from under 30 minutes of human demonstrations.

RoboActu noted that the videos are the company's own communication and that Reward AI did not publish success rates, numbers of attempts, comparisons with other models or customer-site results alongside them. For other recent work on humanoids carrying loads, see our report on Amazon researchers' whole-body carrying study and our research hub.

Analysis

Opinion: Stairs and doorsteps are where wheeled delivery robots struggle, which is why legged demos like this draw attention. Short, company-edited clips show capability, not reliability; repeatable results across many homes and weather conditions would be the real signal.

Why it's trending

  • What happened: on 27 October 2026 Reward AI posted videos on X of humanoid robots running its OM-1 model on parcel delivery from a vehicle and on picking up objects while standing on stairs.
  • Why it is being discussed: the main post had more than 320,000 views within about a day, and the company says OM-1 is trained from human demonstrations captured with a sensor glove rather than robot data (X, Reward AI, RoboActu).
  • What to watch: success rates, number of trials and tests in less prepared settings, which RoboActu notes were not provided with the video.
Note: Capabilities and training-data claims are Reward AI's own and have not been independently verified. View counts were taken from X on 28 October 2026 and will change.
