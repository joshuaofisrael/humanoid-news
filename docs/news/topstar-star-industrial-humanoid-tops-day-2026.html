Topstar unveils Star, a 172 cm industrial humanoid for sorting and material handling

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 13 November 2026 · Last updated 13 November 2026

Chinese industrial robot maker Topstar has unveiled Star, its first full-size bipedal humanoid. The robot was shown at the company's TOPS DAY 2026 open day in Dongguan on 11 November, The Robotics Media reports, citing Pandaily. Star stands 172 cm tall and weighs 72 kg, figures that match Topstar's own release.

Each of its two arms has seven axes and a 10 kg load capacity, with cross-axis wrist joints meant to move more like a human wrist than a conventional industrial arm. Topstar says Star's main jobs will be material handling and sorting, work it already automates with wheeled robots and fixed workstations in customer factories.

Topstar describes the plan as a closed loop of scenario, robot, data and AI: Star is meant to gather physical scene data during real operations and feed it back into the models that control it, rather than relying only on simulation, Gasgoo reports. The humanoid joins a line-up that already includes wheeled sorting robots and Xingzai, a quadruped launched in January 2026 for inspection and forest-firefighting work.

At the same event the company showed the S400, a five-axis machine built by its subsidiary Avmi for precision machining of humanoid robot joints, and announced a procurement agreement with Kaihang Technology. Topstar has not disclosed a mass-production timeline, pricing or initial customers for Star.

The launch adds another factory-focused humanoid to a busy week for Chinese makers, after UBTech's 400-unit U1 Pro order from car dealer Yongda, which we covered on Thursday.
