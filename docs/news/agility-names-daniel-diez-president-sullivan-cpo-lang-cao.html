Two weeks after its Nasdaq debut, Agility reshuffles the top team: Daniel Diez becomes President

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 19 November 2026 · Last updated 19 November 2026

Agility Robotics has named a new President, Chief Product Officer and Chief Administrative Officer. The company posted on X on Wednesday: "Daniel Diez is now President, Christopher “Sully” Sullivan is Chief Product Officer, and Ana Lang is Chief Administrative Officer."

According to Agility's announcement, Diez was previously Chief Business Officer and joined in 2024 as Chief Strategy Officer. As President he leads the whole commercial business. Agility credits him with more than $300 million in committed multi-year orders for Digit 5, which it describes as potential multi-year value subject to contractual milestones and product specifications. Before Agility he held senior roles at Magic Leap, including Chief Commercial Officer.

Sullivan joined in May 2026 as Vice President of Customer Success. He will now run product strategy for Digit, Agility Arc and future products, and keep the customer success team. He previously worked at Automated Tire, Onward Robotics, Berkshire Grey and Symbotic. Lang, previously Chief Legal and People Officer, keeps the legal and people functions, serves as General Counsel and Corporate Secretary, and takes on governance, compliance and risk as Agility operates as a public company.

"Humanoid robots have moved from pilots to real work," Diez said in the release. "As President, my focus is to scale our commercial business: more customers, larger deployments and new partnerships." Chief executive Peggy Johnson said the team is "built to turn demand for Digit into deployments at scale".

The changes come two weeks after Agility completed its merger with Churchill Capital Corp XI on 4 November and began trading on Nasdaq as AGLT. The company points to the expanded GXO deal and three paid manufacturing evaluations and a planned AGCO pilot as the momentum behind the reshuffle. RobotToday notes that Agility gave no timeline for the AGCO pilot or the paid evaluations.
