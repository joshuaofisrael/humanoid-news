"More humanoids on campus than humans": Brett Adcock takes aim at Tesla and China's dancing robots

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 19 November 2026 · Last updated 19 November 2026

Figure founder Brett Adcock says his company has crossed a symbolic line. "We recorded at Figure HQ, we now have more humanoids on campus than humans," he posted on X on Wednesday, announcing a new podcast with Peter Diamandis and Dave Blundin. Figure has not published head counts for either, so this is Adcock's own claim.

The episode is on YouTube as "Robotic Deep-Dive: Beating China, the Race Against Tesla, & New Robotics Timeline With Brett Adcock". Its chapters cover when humanoids will enter homes, robot pricing and "the Figure 4 breakthrough", robots building other robots, and scaling production to millions of robots.

The sharpest moment came when Diamandis asked about the competition. In a clip shared by The Humanoid Hub, Diamandis asks: "As you think about competition, is it Tesla and China?" Adcock answers: "I personally haven't seen much out of Tesla in two or three years. I've seen some teleoperated robots."

On China, Adcock said in the same clip: "I've seen China do a lot of dancing." He argued that the neural network work behind dance demos is "literally 180-degree different work" from what Figure is building, adding that if Figure were told to out-dance China, "we'd have to pivot everybody's strategy".

Tesla did not respond in the clip, and the remarks are one competitor's view of another. Tesla has said it is building a dedicated Optimus factory in Austin; we have covered its delivery timeline before.
