91 football pitches of floor space: drone footage shows how big Tesla's Optimus factory in Austin is getting

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 19 November 2026 · Last updated 19 November 2026

The building Tesla plans to make Optimus in is now big enough to see clearly from the air. "Let's go on a quick 360 degree flight over the Giga Texas Optimus factory!" Giga Texas drone pilot Joe Tegtmeyer posted on X on Wednesday, describing a "massive 7+ million square feet facility" coming together.

Tegtmeyer says basic grade work began in early March, steel assembly started in early June, and "the goal is to have the steel structure fully assembled by the end of March 2027". "Seems on track for that to me," he wrote. He also spotted new foundations on the south east side of the footprint, "possibly" for IDRA Giga Presses for Optimus production. That last point is his guess, not a Tesla statement.

The Humanoid Hub shared the footage with its own comparison: "A 7m sq ft Optimus factory is under rapid construction in Austin. That's 91 soccer pitches." It added that this is more floor space than the entire Fremont factory, which builds the Model Y and Model 3 and houses the first Optimus production line. Those comparisons are the account's, based on the reported size.

Earlier aerial reports point the same way. Texas Construction Hub reported on 3 November that the roughly 3,500 foot long building associated with the Optimus factory had about 60% of its main structural steel assembled, based on site imagery. It noted that Tesla had not confirmed the individual milestones.

Tesla has said it is building a dedicated Optimus factory in Austin, and we have covered its delivery timeline. A finished steel frame is not a working production line: equipment, tooling and hiring come after.
