UBTECH agrees to supply 400 U1 Pro humanoids to car dealer Yongda for its showrooms

By Humanoid News Staff · Published 12 November 2026 · Last updated 12 November 2026

UBTECH signed a strategic cooperation agreement with car dealer Yongda Auto on 11 November under which Yongda will buy 400 of UBTECH's U1 Pro ultra-bionic humanoid robots, Yicai reported, citing UBTECH. UBTECH plans to deliver the robots within this year, according to the report.

The robots are meant for product demonstrations, sales promotion and customer service in Yongda's dealerships, Servospace reported. The two companies also plan to sell and lease robots through car retail, open a UBTECH experience centre in Shanghai and test store-in-store robot displays at selected Yongda outlets in eastern China. Yicai said the partners will also cooperate on industry applications, delivery services and data.

Neither company has disclosed the contract value. At the U1 Pro's list price of RMB 169,800, 400 units would come to about RMB 67.92 million (roughly $10.1 million) at list price, Servospace calculated, noting that this is an estimate and not the deal value. UBTECH told Chinese media it is the first large order it has disclosed for its ultra-bionic line, according to Servospace.

Yongda, a Hong Kong-listed luxury car dealer, describes its robot business in its interim report as being at the incubation and business-model validation stage, with no large-scale revenue yet, Servospace reported.

Analysis

This is a channel deal, not a factory deployment. The buyer is a car retailer putting humanoids in showrooms and planning to resell or lease them, so the order says more about UBTECH building a consumer sales network than about robots doing production work. The real test will be whether Yongda reports any robot resale or leasing revenue.

Note: Order details come from the companies as reported by Yicai and Servospace. The contract value was not disclosed; the list-price figure is an estimate. This is not investment advice.
