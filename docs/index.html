What does HumanoidBeat cover?

HumanoidBeat covers news about humanoid robots from any company or lab, including Tesla Optimus, Figure, Agility Robotics, Boston Dynamics, Unitree, 1X, XPENG and NVIDIA's robotics platforms. Stories cover new robots, AI models, factory and retail pilots, funding announcements, and government standards and safety rules.

Where do your facts come from?

Every article lists its sources at the bottom with links, such as company announcements, regulator releases, research papers and reputable news outlets. We attribute company claims to the company, write in our own words, and leave out anything we cannot trace to a cited source. Read our editorial standards.

How often do you publish?

We publish new humanoid robotics stories on most days, usually several a day when there is real news. The newest stories are at the top of this page and in our RSS feed.
