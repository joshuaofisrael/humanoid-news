Editorial Standards: Sources and Ethics

Sourcing

  • Every factual claim must trace to a named, linkable source (company announcement, regulator or government release, filing, or reputable news outlet) listed at the bottom of the article.
  • We prefer primary sources and say when a claim is only a company's own statement ("according to the company").
  • We do not publish unsourced numbers, dates, or quotes. If we cannot verify something, we leave it out.

Original writing

  • Articles are written in our own words. We use only brief, attributed quotations.

Fairness

  • We do not state or imply wrongdoing by any person or company unless an authoritative source reports it, and then we attribute it.
  • We do not speculate about individuals.

Opinion and analysis

  • Analysis and opinion are clearly labelled as such and kept separate from reported facts.

Company announcements vs. verified claims

  • Statements, figures and timelines that come from a company's own announcement are attributed to that company, and we say when they have not been independently verified.
  • We describe something as confirmed only where an independent source we cite supports it.
  • Targets, forecasts and roadmaps stated by companies are reported as their targets, not as outcomes. We do not promise or imply any result for readers, customers or investors.

Images

  • Images are AI-generated photorealistic scenes, captioned "AI-generated image". They do not depict real identifiable people or real news events.

Not advice

  • Nothing on the site is investment, legal, medical or other professional advice. Articles about funding or listed companies are reports of announcements, not recommendations.

Updates

  • Each article shows its published and last-updated dates. See the corrections policy.

Independence: Humanoid News is not affiliated with the companies it covers unless stated. Ownership details are in our Terms of Use.
