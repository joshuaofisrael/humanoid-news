How this tracker works

  • Each row comes from a HumanoidBeat report, and each report links to its original sources at the bottom.
  • Status describes what the company or the cited outlet said. Company targets and plans are reported as targets, not outcomes, and robot counts are only listed where a source gives them.
  • We add a row when we report a new pilot or deployment, and update a row when the situation changes. Spot something missing or out of date? Tell us.

Common questions

Where are humanoid robots being used today?

In the reports we track, humanoid robots are being trialled or used mainly in car and parts factories (for example in China, Japan, South Korea and the US), in logistics, in one Danish supermarket restocking pilot and in a supervised home-cleaning service in San Francisco. Most are pilots or trials rather than large-scale deployments.

Are these robots replacing workers?

The companies in these reports describe the robots as taking on repetitive or physically demanding tasks. None of the sources we cite report specific job losses, and most projects are still at the pilot stage.

How often is the tracker updated?

We update it when we publish a new report on a humanoid pilot or deployment. The date at the top shows the newest report included.
