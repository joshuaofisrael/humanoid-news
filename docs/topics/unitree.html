Unitree humanoid robot news

Every HumanoidBeat story about Unitree, the Chinese maker of the H1, H2 and G1 humanoids: products, software, pilots and the attention its videos draw. Newest first; each story links to its sources.

8 stories · last updated 29 October 2026

What we know so far

As of 30 October 2026, based on the stories below and their cited sources.

  • Unitree launched the H2-X on 21 October 2026 with a "Kung Fu Legend: Guardian" teaser showing martial-arts moves and staged security and rescue scenes. Unitree gave a maximum endurance of about eight hours (the on-screen text calls it standing time), but no price or release date, and outlets noted it had not said whether the demos were autonomous, remote-controlled or pre-programmed (H2-X video report).
  • At the World Physical AI Conference in Hangzhou, an H2-X lost its balance landing a side flip and its right lower leg came off at the joint, TechNode reported on 26 October. As of that report, Unitree had not commented or explained the cause (Hangzhou demo report).
  • The H2-X is based on the full-size H2, which Unitree describes as about 1.8 metres tall and 70 kg. Unitree's H2 product page lists battery life of about 3 hours and a price of $29,900 before tax and shipping (runtime comparison).
  • Founder Wang Xingxing said on 22 October that Unitree is developing "Unitree Rehearsal", a loop in which AI would write robot code, test it in simulation and then try it on real robots. He called it the company's most important project of the year; no technical details, results or release date were given (Rehearsal report).
  • In Denmark, Føtex is piloting humanoids built on Unitree hardware, with HIVE Robots software, to restock shelves after closing time at a store in Lyngby. HIVE is targeting commercial deployment by early 2027, ESM Magazine reports (Føtex pilot report).
  • The smaller Unitree G1 is a common research platform: ETH Zürich (ladder climbing), an Amazon-led team (whole-body carrying) and Alibaba's Amap team (λ0 model) all tested new methods on it. Their results are the authors' own, from preprints.

Questions readers ask

How much does a Unitree humanoid cost?

Unitree's H2 product page lists the H2 at $29,900 before tax and shipping, as of our 29 October 2026 report. Unitree has not announced a price for the H2-X.

Can you buy the Unitree H2-X?

Not as far as our reports show. Unitree had announced no price or release date for the H2-X when it was unveiled on 21 October 2026.

How long does a Unitree humanoid's battery last?

Unitree lists about 3 hours of battery life for the H2 and says parameters vary with the scenario and configuration. For the H2-X it gave about eight hours, which the video's on-screen text describes as standing time.

What happened to the Unitree H2-X in Hangzhou?

During a demonstration at the World Physical AI Conference in Hangzhou, an H2-X fell while landing a side flip and its right lower leg detached at the joint, TechNode reported. Unitree had not explained the cause as of that report.

Where are Unitree robots being used?

Beyond research labs, the deployment we have reported is a multi-year pilot at a Føtex supermarket in Lyngby, Denmark, where Unitree hardware running HIVE Robots software is being trained to restock shelves after closing time.
