Agility Robotics Digit: news, customers and Digit 5

Agility Robotics' Digit humanoid: who uses it, GXO and AGCO deployments, Digit 5 timing and orders, and every story, each linked to its sources.

4 stories · last updated 29 October 2026

What we know so far

As of 27 October 2026, based on the stories below and their cited sources.

  • Agility says Digit already works with GXO, Schaeffler and Toyota Motor Manufacturing Canada, and that it has added four customers in automotive, consumer goods and agriculture (Digit 5 and Pittsburgh report).
  • GXO, Agility's first commercial customer, has used Digit at Flowery Branch, Georgia, under a multi-year agreement that began in June 2024, and Agility says Digit has moved more than 100,000 totes there. GXO plans two more US sites, Statesville, North Carolina, and Lebanon, Tennessee, from early 2027 (GXO expansion report).
  • AGCO plans a Digit pilot at its Hesston, Kansas, factory from 2027, starting with sorting and stacking parts and placing totes for storage (AGCO pilot report).
  • Digit 5, the next version, will run on NVIDIA IGX Thor onboard computing. Agility expects early access in the first half of 2027, with availability widening over the following six to nine months.
  • Agility says it holds more than $300 million in multi-year Digit 5 orders, subject to contractual milestones and without naming the customers. It describes the figure as potential multi-year value, not current revenue.
  • Three more manufacturers, none named, are in paid programs, and Agility says it is talking to more than 30 prospective customers without signed agreements for those talks.

Questions readers ask

What does the Digit humanoid robot do?

In the deployments Agility has announced, Digit handles repetitive material handling: moving totes in GXO's e-commerce fulfilment sites, and, in AGCO's planned pilot, sorting and stacking parts and placing totes for storage.

When will Digit 5 be available?

Agility says early access to Digit 5 should begin in the first half of 2027, with availability widening over the following six to nine months for manufacturing, warehouse and distribution operators. These are the company's targets.

Is Agility Robotics a public company?

Not as of our latest report. Agility is going public through a merger with the blank-check company Churchill Capital Corp XI, with the proposed ticker AGLT, according to reporting we cite. This is not investment advice.

How many Digit robots are deployed?

Agility has not published a robot count in the announcements we have covered, and GXO's expansion did not say how many robots will go to the new sites.
