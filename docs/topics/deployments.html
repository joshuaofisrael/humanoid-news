Humanoid robots at work: pilots and deployments

HumanoidBeat coverage of humanoid robots being trialled or used in factories, shops and warehouses, and of companies' delivery targets. Newest first; each story links to its sources.

  • Why Tau Robotics' humanoid home-cleaning subscription is trending
    30 October 2026 A 29 October post from Tau Robotics' founder announcing recurring bookings for its supervised humanoid cleaners in San Francisco passed 670,000 views on X within a day. Here is what the service is, according to the company, and what to watch.
  • Audi tests Agile Robots' mobile humanoids for parts sequencing, with Ingolstadt in view
    30 October 2026 Audi, Agile Robots, Dassault Systèmes and NVIDIA are running a proof of concept in Audi's Production Lab in which mobile humanoids pick car parts from containers and place them in assembly order, with the Ingolstadt plant as the target, according to the companies and trade press.
  • ROKAE says it has delivered its 10,000th humanoid force-controlled arm
    26 October 2026 Hong Kong-listed ROKAE Robotics says it has delivered 10,000 humanoid force-controlled arms, and that five of its Helios wheeled humanoids work on Schaeffler's humanoid-parts line in Taicang.
  • Toyota and Honda test humanoid robots on Japanese factory lines, from wire harnesses to final inspection
    26 October 2026 Toyota and Honda are trialling humanoid robots in Japanese plants on tasks including wire-harness installation and final vehicle inspection, according to Automotive News reporting picked up widely on 26 October.
  • FAW-Volkswagen widens UBTECH humanoid trials from inspection to factory logistics
    26 October 2026 FAW-Volkswagen and UBTECH have agreed to develop and test humanoid robots for factory logistics, building on Walker S Lite inspection trials at the carmaker's Qingdao plant. No robot numbers, sites or timetable have been given.
  • AGCO plans a Digit humanoid pilot at its Hesston, Kansas, factory from 2027
    25 October 2026 Farm-machinery maker AGCO and Agility Robotics say they have signed a commercial agreement to pilot the Digit humanoid at AGCO's Hesston, Kansas, plant, starting in 2027.
  • Chinese humanoid makers seek factory and cafe trials in South Korea
    24 October 2026 UBTech, Anyverse Dynamics and other Chinese firms are pursuing trials in South Korea as Seoul proposes 3.1 trillion won for physical AI, Korea Times reports.
  • Tesla targets end of 2027 for external Optimus deliveries, Musk says
    22 October 2026 Elon Musk said on Tesla's Q3 2026 earnings call that Tesla expects to start delivering Optimus robots to outside customers around the end of 2027, according to Electrek.
  • Danish grocer Føtex pilots Unitree humanoids for overnight restocking
    22 October 2026 Føtex, part of Salling Group, has started a multi-year pilot with HIVE Robots to train humanoid robots to restock shelves after closing time, ESM Magazine reports.
  • Schaeffler opens Taicang factory where humanoids help build parts for humanoids
    20 October 2026 Schaeffler says its new Taicang, China factory uses six humanoids to assemble joint modules, including actuators, for other humanoid robots.
  • South Korea grants safety-rule exemption for 20 Robros humanoids in factory pilot
    14 October 2026 A Korean regulatory-sandbox committee granted Robros an exemption so 20 of its humanoid robots can work near people at four companies' sites, Seoul Economic Daily reports.
